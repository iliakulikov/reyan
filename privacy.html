What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (such as your computer, tablet or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve user experience and provide information to website owners.

Similar technologies – such as pixels, tags, local storage and scripts – can perform similar functions. In this Cookie Policy, we refer to all of these technologies collectively as “cookies”.

We use both first-party cookies (set by us) and third-party cookies (set by other domains) for the following purposes:

Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the basic functioning of our Website and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set only in response to actions you take, such as:

Persistent cookies – which remain on your device for a period of time specified in the cookie (for example, days, months or years), unless you delete them earlier.
